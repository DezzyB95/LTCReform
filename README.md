# Together Network — Indiana Long-Term Care Reform

A social media campaign for Indiana long-term care reform, built around one argument:

> Indiana pays more per nursing home resident than most states and staffs those homes
> worse than any other state, because the money is legally allowed to leave the building.

And one frame nobody else in Indiana is running:

> Worker safety and resident safety are the same problem. A resident's right only exists
> if someone can act on it without losing their job.

## The three policy asks

1. A **state minimum staffing standard** — the federal one is repealed and barred from
   enforcement until after September 30, 2034, and Indiana has never had its own.
2. **Spend-down accountability** — Medicaid supplemental payments generated by a nursing
   home must be spent on that home's direct care, reported publicly by facility.
3. **Anti-retaliation protection for frontline caregivers** who report unsafe conditions
   or refuse unsafe assignments.

## Timing

The 2026 short session adjourned February 27, 2026. **2027 is the long budget session** —
where nursing home Medicaid reimbursement is actually decided, and where HEA 1277's move
of long-stay residents back to fee-for-service (effective July 1, 2027) will be argued.

Everything between now and January 2027 is the organizing window. Build the list now so
it can move in January.

## Rules that apply to everything in here

- Every statistic gets a source. No exceptions.
- No facility is named as a wrongdoer.
- No resident information, ever. Nothing photographed inside a building.
- Nobody's story is published without written permission.
- Workers are told to use personal devices and personal email, every time.
