What we collect, and what we do with it

This site is run by the Care Floor Coalition, a volunteer project of Indiana long-term care workers, former workers, and families. It is not a union, a law firm, or a charity, and it does not sell or share data with anyone for any purpose other than delivering the petition and organizing the coalition.

When you sign the petition

  • Collected: printed name, residence address, county, who you are (for example, "a current CNA"), and, if you choose, an email and phone. We also record the date and a hashed form of your connection address to stop bots.
  • Used for: compiling the petition for delivery to members of the Indiana General Assembly. Your address is used to determine your House and Senate district (through the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder) so your signature reaches your own legislators. The petition, with names and addresses, is given to the legislators and committee it is presented to. That is what a petition is.
  • Never: your address is never published on this site or anywhere public. If you tick the box to be listed, we show first name, last initial, role, and county only.
  • Email: if you give one, you receive a receipt with a link to confirm your signature and a link to remove it. We may email you about the petition's progress. No newsletters, no fundraising in the first ninety days.

When you join the coalition

  • Collected: name, personal email or phone, county, role, the type of place you work (never its name), how you want to help, and anything you choose to write.
  • Used for: contacting you about coalition work, from a personal, non-work channel. The member list is kept by the organizer and shown to no one else.

What we ask you not to send

No resident names, initials, room numbers, diagnoses, or incidents that could identify a resident. No photographs taken inside a facility. No documents taken from an employer. No facility named as a wrongdoer. Anything like that is deleted.

Removing your details

Every receipt email contains a personal link that deletes your record. If you did not give an email, contact the coalition and say which name and county to remove. If you also signed a paper sheet, tell us and the line is struck before delivery.

Where the data lives

On the server that runs this site, in a single database, backed up by the organizer. Each signature and sign-up is also emailed to the organizer at the moment it is made. Cookies are not used; the site keeps an unsent draft of the form in your own browser only.

If you work in a facility

Use a personal phone and personal email. Reading this site tells your employer nothing; signing publicly might. Indiana is an at-will state, and the window to file a retaliation complaint with the Indiana Department of Labor after an adverse action is 30 calendar days.
