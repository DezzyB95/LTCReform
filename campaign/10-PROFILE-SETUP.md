## Safety notice — put this on the contact page and repeat it monthly

> **Before you contact us:**
>
> Use your personal phone and personal email. Not facility wifi, not a work device, not a
> work email address.
>
> Following this page publicly is visible to your employer. You can read without
> following.
>
> Please do not send photos taken inside a facility, and do not send resident names,
> room numbers, or medical information. We can't use it and it can put your license and
> your job at risk.
>
> We don't publish anything you send without your written permission. Contacting us
> doesn't create any legal relationship and nothing here is legal advice.
>
> If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. To report to the state: Indiana Department
> of Health, 1-800-246-8909, or file online. Long-Term Care Ombudsman:
> in.gov/ombudsman. Adult Protective Services: 1-800-992-6978.

*Verify each of these numbers by calling or loading the page before you publish them.*

## Operational hygiene

- **Separate email** for the project. Never mix it with personal or case correspondence.
- **Two-factor on everything.** These accounts will eventually annoy someone with
  resources.
- **A second admin** on the Facebook Page as soon as you trust someone. Single-admin pages
  die when one person gets locked out.
- **Back up your content.** Keep this repo as the source of truth; platforms delete
  accounts without warning and without appeal.
- **Never post from facility wifi**, yours or anyone's.
