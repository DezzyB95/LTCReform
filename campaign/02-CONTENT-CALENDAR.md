# 8-Week Launch Calendar

Assumes roughly 4–6 hours per week. Everything is drafted in the post files — this is
sequencing, not new writing.

**Batch rule:** write and schedule the whole week on Sunday. Do not post reactively. The
one thing that kills volunteer campaigns is trying to write a post at 9pm after a shift.

**Posting times (Eastern):** 6:30–7:30am (before day shift), 2:30–3:30pm (shift change),
9:30–11:00pm (after second shift, highest LTC-worker engagement). Never mid-morning.

---

## Week 0 — Setup, no posting

- [ ] Accounts created: Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok, Bluesky, LinkedIn (personal is
      fine), Gmail for the project

      warning included)

- [ ] Build 3 carousels and 3 videos in advance so week 1 doesn't depend on new work

## Week 9+ — Sustainable rhythm

Drop to a repeating weekly template so this never requires invention again:

- **Mon** — The Math (worker)
- **Tue** — Where the Money Went (receipts)
- **Wed** — You're Allowed To (rights)
- **Thu** — Family-facing
- **Fri** — Action / legislature
- **Sat** — LinkedIn or Bluesky (policy audience)
- **Sun** — off, or Stories only

Take Sunday off. A campaign that burns out its only organizer in ten weeks accomplishes
nothing, and burnout is the exact failure mode this entire project exists to name.
