Right now is the cheapest time all year to reach an Indiana legislator.

The General Assembly's 2027 session is the long one. The budget session. It is where Medicaid reimbursement for nursing homes actually gets decided.

Between now and January, legislators are in the interim, doing study committees. Almost nobody is contacting them about long-term care, which means a message right now actually lands.

Finding your legislator takes about four minutes. Link in the first comment.

Three things worth asking for:

1. A state minimum staffing standard, since the federal one is gone until at least 2034.

2. A requirement that Medicaid money generated by a nursing home be spent on that nursing home's direct care, reported publicly by facility.

3. Protection from retaliation for caregivers who report unsafe conditions.

If you would rather not use your name, families can send this. If you are currently working in a facility, think carefully before signing anything that identifies where you work.
