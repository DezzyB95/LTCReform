Here is the part most people don't know.

Over the last fifteen years or so, county-owned hospitals bought up nearly all of Indiana's roughly 500 nursing homes.

Not because a county hospital in one town needed a nursing home four counties away. Because government ownership qualified those homes for a much higher Medicaid reimbursement rate. Around 36% higher than a privately owned facility gets.

The extra money was justified as improving care for residents.

The IndyStar spent years tracking where it actually went. Twenty-one county hospitals kept about 46% of the $5.6 billion those nursing homes generated. More than $2.6 billion.

Meanwhile Indiana's nursing homes fell to 50th in the nation for staffing.

The money worked exactly as designed. It just wasn't designed for the residents.
