Vague complaints get closed. Specific ones get surveyed.

If you are filing about an Indiana facility, include all six of these:

When — date, time, shift.
Who — resident, staff, and who witnessed it.
What — specifically who said or did what.
Outcome — was the resident hurt, afraid, hospitalized, refused a choice?
Where — where it happened, and where the resident is now.
How it came about — what preceded it.

That structure is what the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman's office says makes a complaint verifiable. Use their format and it is much harder to close without a look.

Indiana Department of Health: 1-800-246-8909, or file online.
Long-Term Care Ombudsman: in.gov/ombudsman

You can file anonymously.
