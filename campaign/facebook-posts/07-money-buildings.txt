Here is what the diverted nursing home money actually bought.

New hospital buildings. Renovations. Equipment. Ambulances. Technology.

In Indianapolis, the Health & Hospital Corporation of Marion County drew on funds generated by more than 70 nursing homes to help build the $754 million Sidney & Lois Eskenazi Hospital.

What it did not buy: a second aide on the hall.

Every one of those purchases probably looked reasonable in a board meeting. That is how two and a half billion dollars leaves a system without anyone ever deciding to harm anyone.
