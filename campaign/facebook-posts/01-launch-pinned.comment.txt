Sources for the numbers above:

The $2.6 billion figure and the county hospital ownership arrangement come from the IndyStar's investigation by Tony Cook, Tim Evans and Emily Hopkins — a continuation of their 2020 "Careless" series, published March 2025. They had to sue eight county hospitals in 2022 to get public records about how public money was spent.

The 50th-in-the-nation ranking is an IndyStar analysis of federal payroll-based staffing data, adjusted for resident acuity. Indiana came in at 3.51 total nurse staffing hours per resident day against a national average of 3.76, falling short in all three roles.

Q1 2026 federal data from the Long Term Care Community Coalition shows Indiana staffing 29.6% below its residents' expected need, among the ten worst states in the country.
