Let's do the math out loud.

Twenty residents. One aide. Eight hours.

That's 480 minutes. Subtract report at the start and end of shift. Subtract charting. Subtract two meal passes. Subtract the fifteen minutes you spend looking for the one working Hoyer lift on the floor.

You are left with somewhere under 20 minutes per resident, for the entire shift.

In those 20 minutes you are expected to do two rounds of incontinence care. Repositioning. A shower if it's their day. Getting them up. Feeding, if they need help. Answering the call light.

And being a human being to someone who may not have had a visitor in three weeks.

Twenty minutes.

Nobody is failing at this job. The job is arithmetically impossible, and it was made that way on a spreadsheet by someone who has never done it.
