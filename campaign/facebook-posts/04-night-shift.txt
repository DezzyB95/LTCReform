Third shift. Forty residents. One aide, one nurse.

Rounds every two hours.

Do that math and it comes out to about three minutes a person — assuming nobody falls, nobody needs a full change, nobody is having a bad night, and nobody is dying.

Someone always is.

Statewide, Indiana nursing homes staff nearly 30 percent below what their own residents' assessed needs call for. That is not one bad building. That is the whole state.
