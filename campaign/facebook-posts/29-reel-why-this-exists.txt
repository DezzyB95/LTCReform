I tried the state. I tried IOSHA. I tried the ombudsman. I tried CMS.

I filed the complaints you're supposed to file. The conditions didn't change.

What I figured out is that resident advocacy and worker advocacy in Indiana are treated as two separate things, and that is exactly why neither one works.

You cannot ask someone to protect a resident's rights when protecting those rights costs them their job.

So this is a project for both at once.
