Where the money went. Six slides.

Indiana ranks 50th in the nation for nursing home staffing.

Over about fifteen years, county hospitals acquired nearly all of Indiana's roughly 500 nursing homes. Government ownership qualified those homes for a Medicaid rate about 36% higher than privately owned facilities get.

Those homes generated about $5.6 billion. Twenty-one county hospitals kept about 46% of it — more than $2.6 billion.

It paid for hospital buildings, renovations, equipment, ambulances, technology.

The money came in for nursing home care. It left before it reached the floor.

Source: IndyStar "Careless" investigation.
