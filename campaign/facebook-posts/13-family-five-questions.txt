Five questions to ask an Indiana nursing home. Ask them in writing.

If you have someone in a facility, these are worth asking and worth keeping the answer to.

1. How many aides are assigned to my parent's hall on each shift?

2. What was that number last weekend, and overnight?

3. How many of the aides working today are agency staff who have never met them?

4. What is the turnover rate on this unit this year?

5. Who do I contact if the answer to any of these changes?

Ask by email so there is a record.

A facility that staffs well answers these easily. A facility that doesn't will tell you the numbers aren't available. They are available.

Staffing data is also public on Medicare's Care Compare site. You do not have to take anyone's word for it.
