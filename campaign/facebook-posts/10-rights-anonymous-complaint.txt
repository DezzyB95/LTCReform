You can file a complaint about an Indiana nursing home anonymously.

Indiana Department of Health: 1-800-246-8909, or online.

You can also contact the Long-Term Care Ombudsman. Their office does not release a complainant's name without permission.

Two honest caveats.

Anonymous does not always mean unidentifiable. If you were the only one there, the facility can often work it out.

And a complaint only goes anywhere if it is specific. When it happened — date, time, shift. Who was involved and who witnessed it. Specifically who said or did what. What the outcome was. Where it happened and where the resident is now.

Vague complaints get closed. Specific ones get surveyed.
