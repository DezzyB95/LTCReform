Your name is protected by state law when you file a complaint in Indiana.

The Indiana Department of Health does not identify or release the name of the person who submits a complaint. That is not a courtesy, it is state law.

The Long-Term Care Ombudsman's office also does not release a complainant's name without permission.

Two honest caveats.

Confidential does not always mean unidentifiable. If you were the only one there, the facility can often work it out.

And a complaint only goes anywhere if it is specific. When it happened — date, time, shift. Who was involved and who witnessed it. Specifically who said or did what. What the outcome was. Where it happened and where the resident is now.

At minimum you need the facility's name and the city it is in. You do not need to have every answer to file.

Vague complaints get closed. Specific ones get surveyed.
