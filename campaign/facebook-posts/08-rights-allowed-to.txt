Things Indiana long-term care workers are allowed to do, that people are regularly told they can't:

You're allowed to talk to your coworkers about staffing.

You're allowed to talk to your coworkers about your pay.

You're allowed to talk about scheduling, safety, and working conditions.

You're allowed to do all of that together, as a group.

Federal labor law protects most private-sector employees acting together over working conditions, whether or not there is a union anywhere near your building.

Protected does not mean nobody will retaliate. It means retaliation is illegal and you can file on it.

Which is exactly why you document everything. Dates, times, what was said. On your own phone, off the clock.

General information, not legal advice.
