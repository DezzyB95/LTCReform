To families: the aide caring for your mother is on your side.

She sees what you see. She is not allowed to say it.

When you ask hard questions about staffing, you are asking them on behalf of someone who would be fired for asking. That is not an exaggeration. It is the most common story I hear.

Families can say out loud what workers can't.

That is the most useful thing you have, and most families never realize they have it.
