Indiana Department of Labor, IOSHA Whistleblower Protection Unit:
https://www.in.gov/dol/iosha/whistleblower-protection-unit/

Federal OSHA has the same 30-day window under Section 11(c). You can file online:
https://www.whistleblowers.gov/complaint_page

When you file, be specific: dates, what you reported, who you reported it to, and what happened to you afterward.
