The timeline, if you want to check it:

CMS final rule, May 2024 — 3.48 total nurse hours per resident day, 0.55 RN, 2.45 nurse aide, RN on site 24/7.

Vacated by a federal court in the Northern District of Texas, April 7, 2025.

Section 71111 of Public Law 119-21 bars CMS from implementing or enforcing those standards until after September 30, 2034.

CMS interim final rule repealing the standards published December 3, 2025, effective February 2, 2026:
https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/03/2025-21792/medicare-and-medicaid-programs-repeal-of-minimum-staffing-standards-for-long-term-care-facilities
