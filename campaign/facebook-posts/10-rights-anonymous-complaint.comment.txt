Indiana Department of Health complaint line: 1-800-246-8909 during state business hours. If that line is busy or you are calling after hours, the voicemail line is 317-233-7241. You can also file online.

Within 7 to 10 business days of filing, you should receive a letter confirming the complaint was received and will be investigated. If you don't, follow up.

Long-Term Care Ombudsman: in.gov/ombudsman

Adult Protective Services: 1-800-992-6978

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
