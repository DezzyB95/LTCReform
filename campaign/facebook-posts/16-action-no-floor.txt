Something that got very little coverage in Indiana and should have gotten a lot.

In 2024 the federal government set a minimum staffing standard for nursing homes. 3.48 total nurse hours per resident per day, and a registered nurse in the building 24 hours a day.

A federal court struck it down in April 2025.

Then Congress blocked it from being enforced until after September 30, 2034.

In February 2026 it was formally repealed.

Indiana has never had a state minimum of its own.

So as of right now, there is no floor. None. For at least the next eight years, whatever Indiana decides is the only thing that exists.

That is not a reason to give up. It is the entire reason state law is now the only thing that matters.
