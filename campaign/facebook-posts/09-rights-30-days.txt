If you are fired, demoted, cut in hours, or written up after reporting a safety problem in Indiana, you have 30 calendar days to file a retaliation complaint with the Indiana Department of Labor.

Thirty calendar days. Not thirty business days. Not thirty days after HR gets back to you.

Most people lose that window without ever knowing it existed, because they spend the first month going through HR and internal appeals.

The clock does not stop for that.

File first. Appeal internally at the same time if you want to. But file.
