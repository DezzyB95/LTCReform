Things families notice that usually mean short staffing, not bad aides:

Call lights ringing a long time.

Someone still in bed at 11am who used to get up.

Weight loss.

New skin breakdown.

Being wet when you arrive, consistently, at the same time of day.

A completely new face every single visit.

The aide standing in front of you probably knows all of it and cannot say it. She is likely carrying 15 to 20 people and has been told to move faster.

Direct the question up. Ask the administrator, in writing, how many staff were assigned to that hall.
