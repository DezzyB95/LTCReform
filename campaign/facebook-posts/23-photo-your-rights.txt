Things Indiana long-term care workers are allowed to do:

Talk to coworkers about staffing. Talk to coworkers about pay. Talk about scheduling, safety, and working conditions. Do all of it together, as a group.

Most private-sector workers are protected acting together over working conditions, union or not.

Protected does not mean nobody retaliates. It means retaliation is illegal and you can file on it.

So document everything. Dates, times, names. Your own phone, off the clock.

General information, not legal advice.
