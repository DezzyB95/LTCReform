# Sources

Every factual claim in this campaign, with its source. Re-verify anything older than six

## Medicaid diversion / county hospital ownership

- IndyStar, "Careless" investigation (Emily Hopkins, Tony Cook). County hospitals acquired
  nearly all of Indiana's ~500 nursing homes; 21 public hospitals retained ~46% of $5.6B
  generated, over $2.6B diverted; spent on hospital buildings, renovations, equipment,
  ambulances, technology; IndyStar sued 8 county hospitals in 2022 for the records.
  - https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/post-acute/indiana-hospitals-divert-2-6b-in-medicaid-from-nursing-homes-report/
  - https://www.newsweek.com/2-6-billion-medicaid-funds-diverted-indiana-care-homes-report-2046425
  - https://journalistsresource.org/health/nursing-homes-dollars-diverted-county-hospitals-indiana/
- Peer-reviewed background on Indiana's IGT-based supplemental payments:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10330393/
- Government-owned facilities qualify for a substantially higher rate (~36% above
  privately owned). Per the reporting above.

## Staffing rankings

- Indiana ranks **50th of 50 states + D.C.** for total nurse staffing hours per resident,
  acuity-adjusted. IndyStar analysis of federal payroll-based journal data.
- **88%** of Indiana nursing homes staffed below residents' assessed clinical needs;
  Indiana averages **0.66 RN hours per resident day**; nearly 9 in 10 U.S. nursing homes
  understaffed (Q1 2026). Long Term Care Community Coalition:
  https://nursinghome411.org/alert-staffing-q1-2026/
- Indiana ranked 48th on AARP's LTSS scorecard for choice of options/providers:
  https://www.aarp.org/states/indiana/

## Federal minimum staffing rule — repealed

- CMS final rule, May 2024: 3.48 total HPRD, 0.55 RN, 2.45 nurse aide, RN onsite 24/7.
  https://www.kff.org/medicaid/a-closer-look-at-the-final-nursing-facility-rule-and-which-facilities-might-meet-new-staffing-requirements/
- Vacated April 7, 2025, N.D. Tex. (Judge Kacsmaryk).
  https://www.modernhealthcare.com/providers/nursing-home-staffing-mandate-struck-down/
  https://www.mcknights.com/news/breaking-judge-tosses-nursing-home-staffing-rule/
- **Section 71111 of P.L. 119-21** bars implementation/enforcement until after
  **September 30, 2034**. Administration dismissed its appeal October 2025.
  https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/insights/insights/minimum-staffing-rules-for-long-term-care-facilities-tossed-out-by-federal-courts-and-budget-bill
- CMS interim final rule repealing the standards, published December 3, 2025, effective
  **February 2, 2026**.
  https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/03/2025-21792/medicare-and-medicaid-programs-repeal-of-minimum-staffing-standards-for-long-term-care-facilities
  https://theconsumervoice.org/news/cms-takes-action-to-rescind-minimum-staffing-rule/

## Retaliation and worker rights

- **IOSHA retaliation complaints: 30 calendar days** to the Indiana Department of Labor.
  https://www.in.gov/dol/iosha/whistleblower-protection-unit/
- Federal OSHA §11(c): also 30 days. Fact sheet 3812:
  https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3812.pdf
  File online: https://www.whistleblowers.gov/complaint_page
- Indiana is an at-will employment state.
- NLRA §7 protects most private-sector employees engaged in concerted activity over wages,
  hours, and working conditions, regardless of union status. Supervisors generally
  excluded.

- Ombudsman guidance on what makes a complaint verifiable — when (day, time, shift), who
  (and who witnessed), what specifically was said or done, the outcome, where it happened
  and where the resident is now, and what preceded it. Complaints may be filed
  anonymously; the Ombudsman program does not release a complainant's name without
  permission.
- **Adult Protective Services: 1-800-992-6978** — verify before publishing.
